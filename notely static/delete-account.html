Delete Your Notely Account

You can permanently delete your Notely account and all the data associated with it. There are two ways to do it.

01

In the App Instant

  1. Open Notely and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  4. Type DELETE to confirm

Your account and data are removed immediately.

02

By Email If app uninstalled

Email socializeme11@gmail.com from the address your account uses — we need that to confirm the request is really yours — with the subject:

"Delete my account"

We action requests within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you once it's done.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile — name, email address, chosen course
  • Your saved notes and bookmarks
  • Your record of which note you unlocked for free
  • Your active device session
  • Your purchase records held by us
  • Your login credentials

What Is Not Deleted

  • Google Play transaction records. Google keeps its own record of purchases for its accounting and tax obligations. That is under Google's control, not ours — see Google's Privacy Policy ↗.
⚠️

This cannot be undone. Deletion is permanent. Any remaining Premium time is lost and is not refunded. If you sign up again later you start fresh, including the one-free-note allowance.
