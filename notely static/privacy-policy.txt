PRIVACY POLICY — NOTELY
Last updated: 15 August 2026

This policy explains what Notely ("the app", "we") collects, why, and what
control you have. It covers the Notely Android app and its backend.

IN SHORT: we collect the minimum needed to run an account and a paid pass —
your name, email, chosen course, and a random device identifier. We do not sell
your data, we do not run ads or advertising trackers, and we do not collect your
location, contacts, photos, or files.


1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

Notely is operated by the developer of the Notely app ("we"). For any privacy
question or request, contact us at socializeme11@gmail.com. We aim to reply
within 30 days.


2. WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY

Email address
  Why: To create and sign you in to your account, send password resets, and
  contact you about your account.
  Source: You, at sign-up (or your Google account if you use Google Sign-In).

Name
  Why: Shown in the app and watermarked on notes you open, to discourage
  redistribution of paid material.
  Source: You, at sign-up (or your Google account).

Chosen course / branch
  Why: To show you the right subjects and notes.
  Source: You, in the app.

A random device identifier
  Why: To enforce one active device per account, which protects paid content
  from account sharing.
  Source: Generated randomly by the app and stored on your device. It is NOT
  your hardware ID, IMEI, advertising ID, or any identifier that can track you
  across other apps.

Device type and OS version
  Why: Shown so you can recognise which device holds your active session.
  Source: Your device.

Purchase records
  Why: To grant and track your Premium pass, and to support refund queries.
  Source: Google Play, when you buy a pass. We receive a purchase token and
  order ID. WE NEVER SEE OR STORE YOUR CARD, UPI ID, OR BANK DETAILS.

Saved notes (bookmarks) and which single note you unlocked for free
  Why: To provide the library feature and to apply the one-free-note rule.
  Source: Your activity in the app.


WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

  - Location of any kind
  - Contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, camera or microphone data
  - Files or documents from your device
  - Advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs
  - Third-party analytics or behavioural tracking
  - Payment card, UPI, or bank details (these stay with Google Play)


3. LEGAL BASIS

We process this data to perform the contract you enter into when you create an
account and buy a pass, and on the basis of our legitimate interest in
preventing unauthorised sharing of paid study material.


4. WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising. We use
these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy)
  Purpose: Hosts our database, authentication and file storage.
  Data involved: All account data listed above.

Google Play (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  Purpose: Processes purchases and verifies them.
  Data involved: Purchase token, order ID. Payment details are handled entirely
  by Google and never reach us.

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to, or to investigate
suspected abuse of paid content.


5. WHERE DATA IS STORED

Our database and file storage are hosted by Supabase in the Asia-Pacific
(Sydney) region. If you use the app from outside that region, your data is
transferred there.


6. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

  - Account data — until you delete your account.
  - Purchase records — deleted with your account. Google Play keeps its own
    transaction records independently, under its own policy.
  - Device session — replaced whenever you sign in on a new device, and removed
    when you sign out.


7. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT AND DATA

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  - In the app: Profile > Delete my account. You will be asked to type DELETE
    to confirm.
  - If you have uninstalled the app: use our account deletion request page.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. It removes your profile, saved notes,
device session and purchase records from our systems. Any remaining Premium time
is forfeited and is not refundable. Notes downloaded to your device are removed
from the app's private storage when you sign out or delete your account.


8. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export,
or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You
can exercise most of these directly in the app, or by emailing
socializeme11@gmail.com.


9. SECURITY

We protect your data with: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest;
row-level security in the database, so one account cannot read another's data;
time-limited signed links for note files, which expire after two minutes; and
one-active-device enforcement. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these
measures seriously and review them.


10. CHILDREN

Notely is intended for college students and is not directed at children under
13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a
child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.


11. CHANGES

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant
changes, notify you in the app. Continuing to use Notely after a change means
you accept the updated policy.


12. CONTACT

Questions, requests or complaints: socializeme11@gmail.com
